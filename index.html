The Morse tree

Every letter sits on a branch. Start at the root, step up for a dot and down for a dash, and the path you took is the code. Tap any circle to jump there. Keys: . and - to step, Backspace to go back.

Mash it apart

Real Morse leaves a gap between letters. Here the gaps are mashed away, so you get one long string. Tap between symbols to place dividers until every chunk is a letter and the chunks use the hidden letters.
